About This Character

Steve Rogers dons the navy-and-grey "stealth suit" throughout Captain America: The Winter Soldier (2014), the film that introduces Bucky Barnes' brainwashed alter ego and reshapes the MCU's spy-thriller tone. This 1/10 statue depicts Captain America standing, shield in hand, in that suit.
