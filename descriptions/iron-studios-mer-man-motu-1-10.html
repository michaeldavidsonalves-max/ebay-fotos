About This Piece

One of Skeletor's earliest and most loyal generals, Mer‑Man rules the Sea of Rakash and commands the aquatic creatures of Eternia's oceans. A hulking amphibious warrior with gills, claws and a golden trident, he brings the terror of the deep to the Evil Warriors' ranks.

Iron Studios sculpts him mid-lunge, bursting out of the water onto a jagged, coral-encrusted rock formation — a dynamic, highly detailed piece for any Masters of the Universe collection.
