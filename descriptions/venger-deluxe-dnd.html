About This Character

Venger, the Force of Evil, is the main antagonist of the series and, as later revealed, the Dungeon Master's own son. An evil wizard of immense power, Venger seeks to seize the children's magical weapons to destroy Tiamat and conquer the Realm. This Deluxe statue depicts him mounted on a Nightmare — a malevolent, ebony-black steed with a mane and tail of pure hellfire — alongside the Shadow Demon, his personal spy and assistant who keeps him informed of the children's every move.
