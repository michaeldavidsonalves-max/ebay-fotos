About This Piece

Peni Parker is the pilot of SP//dr, a towering psychically-bonded mech suit from an alternate reality where Spider-Man's legacy was passed down through generations of her family. Introduced to mainstream audiences in Spider‑Man: Into the Spider‑Verse, she brought a wholly original fighting style — and robot — to the Spider-Verse.

Iron Studios captures her leaping into action atop SP//dr's shell, one arm raised in triumph as the mech's own clawed limb strikes forward, all set on a jagged rock-formation base true to the film's distinctive animated look.
